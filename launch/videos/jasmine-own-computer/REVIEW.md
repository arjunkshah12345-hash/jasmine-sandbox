# Review: "its own computer" launch film

`npx hyperframes check`: 0 errors, 0 warnings. Rendered 29.2 s, frames reviewed at every beat.

Built against the lessons.sh launch-video notes:
- Hook in the first 3 s, on a problem people feel: your AI agent runs `rm -rf ~/` on your laptop. No logo until 24.8 s.
- The problem in plain words, one line at a time ("Your laptop? Scary." "Someone's cloud? Slow.").
- One core feature: its own computer, ready in a tenth of a second, shown as a race (real medians from tryjasmine.dev, 4× slow motion, sourced on screen). Naming competitors is the controversy.
- Real product: the captured tryjasmine.dev hero, cursor clicks copy on the install command.
- Something odd at the end: a kid pops out of a literal sandbox and says "hi".

Fixed during review:
- The hook chat was too small for a phone; the window now fills the frame and the text is ~40% larger.
- "STOP STOP STOP" went off the bottom under the camera lean; raised.
- The kid's "hi!" covered the end line; moved.
- Problem lines rebalanced so each holds long enough to read; the end card got 0.6 s more for "hi!".
- A cut click sound was silent (MP3 padding); recut to WAV.
